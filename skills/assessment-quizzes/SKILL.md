name: assessment-quizzes
description: >-
  Writes and reviews quizzes in a course: comprehension checks after a reading
  section and placement quizzes at the start of a course. In-course quizzes
  only cover material already taught, test understanding of the Cloudflare
  product rather than recall of facts, and re-explain missed answers in a new
  way instead of pointing back to the section. Placement quizzes go from basic
  to advanced, stay short, and let the learner skip or stop. Use when
  creating, revising or assessing quiz questions, knowledge checks, or
  pre-course assessments.
---

If the quiz is part of a learning course (such as after a reading section):
- Never ask a question about something not yet taught. Ask questions that can deepen understanding of the given topic.
- Don't make the questions just about whether the person remembers some facts.  For example, if numbers are involved, you can repeat the numbers as part of the question.
- If a question is missed, don't just point back to relevant section. Elicit other ways of presenting the information (such as asking for more examples, asking if they want it in comparison to something they are familiar with, etc.)

If the quiz is just used to assess the person's knowledge at the beginning of a course, then, assume things have been taught and start with degrees of difficulty (basic knowledge to deeper knowledge), but don't make it too long. Let the learner skip a question or stop the quiz at any time.
