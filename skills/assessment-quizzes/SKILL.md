---
name: assessment-quizzes
description: Use this skill when to creating or assessing quizzes.
---

If the quiz is part of a learning course (such as after a reading section):
- Never ask a question about something not yet taught. Ask questions that can deepen understanding of the given topic.
- Don't make the questions just about whether the person remembers some facts.  For example, if numbers are involved, you can repeat them as part of the question.
- If a question is missed, don't just point back to relevant section. Elicit other ways of presenting the information (such as asking for more examples, asking if they want it in comparison to something they are familiar with, etc.)

If the quiz is just used to assess the person's knowledge at the beginning of a course, then, assume things have been taught and start with degrees of difficulty (basic knowledge to deeper knowledge), but don't make it too long. And give option to just skip or stop.
